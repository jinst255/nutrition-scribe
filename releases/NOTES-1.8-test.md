**A beta build.** Nutrition Scribe is a food log you talk to: say, type or scan what you ate, and it works out the nutrition. Android 8 or later.

### Install
1. Download **NutritionScribe-1.8-test.apk** below, on your phone.
2. Tap it. Your phone asks whether to allow installs from this source: allow it, go back, tap **Install**.
3. Open it. It asks your name and what to track. An AI key is optional: without one the app works offline with its built-in food list and label database.

Already have an earlier test version? Install this over it; your log is kept.

### New in 1.8
- **Fix it with AI:** on a logged meal, "Something's wrong with this entry?" › say what's wrong › Fix it. You can undo it.
- **Ask** shows its answer as it's written, says what it's doing, and (with an OpenAI key and Search the web on) can look up restaurant and packaged foods online.
- **Speech to text** happens on the phone by default: words appear as you talk, nothing is sent. OpenAI is a choice in Settings › Microphone.
- Saying a change like "that bacon is the low-sodium bacon" or "it was Great Value" now changes that food instead of adding a new one.
- Scanning a label for a food replaces it with what's on the label, keeping your amount.
- Log it while the AI is still checking waits for the AI's answer.
- "Did you have…?" has a plain **No**; Settings ends with **Leave beta testing**.
- Many smaller fixes: typed numbers scale with the amount, "the brand is X" is read as a brand, Ask no longer gets stuck after a dropped connection.

Shake your phone when something looks wrong to leave a note (Settings › Beta testing).
