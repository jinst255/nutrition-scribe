**A beta build.** Nutrition Scribe is a food log you talk to: say, type or scan what you ate, and it works out the nutrition. Android 8 or later; the watch app needs Wear OS 3 or later.

### Install
1. On your phone, download **NutritionScribe-1.9-test.apk** below.
2. Tap it. Your phone asks whether to allow installs from this source: allow it, go back, tap **Install**.
3. Open it. It asks your name and what to track. An AI key is optional: without one the app works offline from its built-in food list and label database.

Already have an earlier test version? Install this over it; your log is kept.

**Watch (optional):** **NutritionScribe-Watch-1.9-test.apk** is the Wear OS app. Sideload it with `adb` (Wireless debugging on the watch, then `adb install -r`). The link between phone and watch hasn't been tried on a real paired phone and watch yet, so tell us how it goes.

### New in 1.9
- **The watch app, rebuilt.** Say what you ate, check the foods, Log it, and you're back on the watch home with Undo. Log again with your usual meals first. What the watch home shows is your pick in Settings › Watch.
- **Watch-face shortcuts:** Need most + Say, Usual meal now, Again, Close the gap, Since you logged and Waiting on phone. Press and hold your watch face › Customise › a slot › Nutrition Scribe.
- **From your watch:** anything too tricky for the watch, or said while your phone was away, waits on the phone. "Work it out with AI" splits it into foods for you to check.
- **Your usual meals:** foods you log together on different days become one tap in Log again.
- **Say a barcode:** "barcode" and then its numbers, on the phone or the watch.
- **Home:** Today comes before Log again, Log again is a grid with each food's calories, and "All your foods" searches everything you've logged. Tap a goal to see its last two weeks.
- **History:** each nutrient against the two weeks before (▲ / ▼ %), and Over time shows your average, your goal and your biggest sources.
- **An entry:** "Something to change? Say or type it" sits right under the total, and the ⋮ menu has Log again now, Change the time, Fix it, Rename it and Delete (with Undo).
- **My foods:** Foods, Labels and Meals; hold a food to log it, edit it, see when you ate it, or delete it.
- **Store foods:** real labels for the stores you shop at (Settings › Store foods), from Open Food Facts.
- **Scanning** keeps the camera and its buttons on screen without scrolling. **About** is at the end of Settings. Text and buttons are bigger.
- Fixes: "2 eggs" said "2 slice" and toast "1 cubic inch" on the check card; every first-time food logged from the watch landed back in a list to check.

Shake your phone when something looks wrong to leave a note (Settings › Beta testing).
