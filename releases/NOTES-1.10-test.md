**A beta build.** Nutrition Scribe is a food log you talk to: say, type or scan what you ate, and it works out the nutrition. Android 8 or later; the watch app needs Wear OS 3 or later.

### Install
1. On your phone, download **NutritionScribe-1.10-test.apk** below.
2. Tap it. Your phone asks whether to allow installs from this source: allow it, go back, tap **Install**.
3. Open it. It asks your name and what to track. An AI key is optional: without one the app works offline from its built-in food list and label database.

Already have an earlier test version? Install this over it; your log is kept. **This is the last one you need to install by hand:** from 1.10 the app updates itself.

**Watch (optional):** the watch app hasn't changed since 1.9; **NutritionScribe-Watch-1.9-test.apk** is attached. Sideload it with `adb` (Wireless debugging on the watch, then `adb install -r`). The link between phone and watch hasn't been tried on a real paired phone and watch yet, so tell us how it goes.

### New in 1.10
- **Updates come to you.** When a new version is out, a card on Home says so. Tap Update: it downloads from GitHub, checks it's really Nutrition Scribe, and Android installs it. Your log, goals and foods stay.
- The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from Nutrition Scribe and to confirm the update. After that, newer phones update without the extra box.
- Settings › Updates: Check for updates, What's new, and a switch to stop it looking by itself (it looks when you open the app, twice a day at most).
- Everything from 1.9: the watch app and its watch-face shortcuts, From your watch, usual meals, say a barcode, the new Home, History, entry page and My foods, and store foods.

Shake your phone when something looks wrong to leave a note (Settings › Beta testing).
